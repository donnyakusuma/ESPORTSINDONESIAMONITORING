Privacy Policy

Esports Indonesia Athlete Monitoring (prototype) · Effective 23 November 2026

1. Who we are

Esports Indonesia Athlete Monitoring is an internal tool used by the national esports team's coaching and support staff to monitor athlete readiness, recovery, and wellbeing. It is not offered to the general public.

2. Data we collect

  • From WHOOP, only after the athlete authorizes access: recovery (recovery score, HRV, resting heart rate), sleep, workouts, daily strain (cycles), basic profile (user ID, name, email), and body measurements.
  • From Garmin Connect, only after the athlete connects their account: daily health statistics such as heart rate, sleep, stress, Body Battery, and calories.
  • Entered in the app: morning check-in answers, training load, reports, and coach notes.

3. How we use it

Data is used only to show each athlete's readiness and wellbeing to the athlete and to the team staff responsible for them, and to adjust training load. We do not sell data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties outside the team.

4. Storage and security

Wearable data and access tokens are stored on the team's own server. OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM). Client secrets stay on the server and are never sent to the browser. Access is limited to authorized team staff.

5. Retention and deletion

An athlete can disconnect WHOOP or Garmin at any time from the app. Disconnecting revokes our access at WHOOP and deletes the stored tokens and synced records. Athletes can also revoke access from their WHOOP account settings. Data is kept only while the athlete is part of the team program.

6. Your rights

Athletes may request to see, correct, or delete their data by contacting the team manager.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the Esports Indonesia national team management.
